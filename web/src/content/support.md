# Rivolt support

Rivolt is a free, open-source companion for Rivian owners, run by an individual. It is unofficial and not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Rivian.

## Get help

Email [support@rivolt.dev](mailto:support@rivolt.dev) with what you were doing and what you saw. Please leave out your Rivian password: nobody at Rivolt will ever ask for it. A reply usually takes a day or two.

For a bug or a feature idea you are happy to discuss in public, open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/apohor/rivolt/issues).

## Common questions

- **I don't see my truck.** Connect your Rivian account on the web, in Settings, Account. The iPhone app shows your truck once it is connected. Try the [demo truck](https://demo.rivolt.dev) to see what Rivolt does without an account.
- **How do I delete my account?** In Settings, Data, Danger zone, on the web, or from the account menu in the iPhone app. Deleting removes your drives, charges and settings from Rivolt.
- **Where is my data, and who sees it?** The [Privacy policy](/privacy) says what Rivolt records, how long it keeps it and how to delete it. The [Terms](/terms) cover the rest.
- **How do I disconnect Rivolt from my Rivian?** Sign out of Rivian in Settings, or remove the Authorized Driver in the Rivian app. [Connecting, in plain terms](/safe-to-connect) explains what to expect.
