# Support

Rivolt is run by ZeroDowntime LLC. Email [support@rivolt.dev](mailto:support@rivolt.dev); we aim to reply within a few days. Requests about your data are handled within 30 days, as the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) says. Include the page or screen you were on, never your password.

Bug reports and feature requests are also welcome on GitHub: [github.com/apohor/rivolt/issues](https://github.com/apohor/rivolt/issues) (public; leave out your VIN, address, location and email address).

## Common questions

**What is Rivolt?**
A free, open-source companion for Rivian owners. It records your truck's drives and charges, prices every charge at your own electricity rate, and shows where each figure came from. Estimates are labelled as estimates. It works on the web at rivolt.dev and as an iPhone app.

**Is it official?**
No. Rivolt is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Rivian. It reads data through Rivian's unofficial API, which Rivian can change or block at any time, so Rivolt can break.

**How do I connect my truck?**
Sign in to rivolt.dev and add your Rivian account on the Connect step. We recommend adding a second Rivian account as an Authorized Driver and connecting that one, so you can remove Rivolt from the Rivian app at any time. Your Rivian password is used once to sign in and is never stored; what we keep is your Rivian email and an encrypted session token. Details: [rivolt.dev/safe-to-connect](/safe-to-connect).

**Does Rivolt wake my truck?**
Rivolt sends no commands to your truck (no locks, climate or charging controls). Reading its data can occasionally wake it.

**How do I export or delete my data?**
On rivolt.dev, open Settings → Data: download everything as a zip of CSV files or a full JSON backup, or delete your account. Deleting removes your data; copies can remain in logs for up to 90 days and in backups for up to a year, as the Privacy Policy describes. The iPhone app links to the same page.

**I connected, but nothing shows up.**
The usual cause is a driver account whose invite has not been accepted yet: sign in to the Rivian app on a phone with that account once, then tap Check again in Rivolt. Drives and charges appear as the truck is used.
