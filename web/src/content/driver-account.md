# Set up an Authorized Driver account for Rivolt

Rivolt reads your truck's data through a Rivian sign-in. Instead of giving it your own login, you can create a second, free Rivian account, add it to your vehicle as an Authorized Driver, and connect that one. Rivolt then holds that account's session, not yours, and you can cut Rivolt off at any time by removing the driver in the Rivian app. It takes a few minutes. Rivolt is unofficial and not affiliated with Rivian.

## Why bother

- To remove Rivolt, you remove the driver in the Rivian app. You don't need to change your own password.
- Rivolt's requests count against the dedicated account, not your own.
- Rivolt never holds your own Rivian login.

Trade-offs: you need a second email address and a few minutes in the Rivian app, and an Authorized Driver account may not see all of the history your own account holds, such as charges the owner started. Live data from the moment you connect is complete. Rivian can change or block access at any time, with either kind of account. You can still connect your own account if you prefer: [Is it safe to connect?](/safe-to-connect) explains what Rivolt keeps.

## Steps

1. Create a new Rivian account at rivian.com with a different email from your own. A plus address works, for example yourname+rivolt@gmail.com.
2. In the Rivian app on your phone, signed in as your own account, open Vehicle, then Settings, then Authorized Drivers, and add the new email.
3. Open the invite email in the new account's inbox and accept it.
4. Important: sign in to the Rivian app on a phone with the new account. Until the new account signs in to the app at least once, the invite stays in "Sent" status and the vehicle is not added, so Rivolt will not see it.
5. Check that the new account shows your vehicle in the Rivian app.
6. Back in Rivolt, sign in to Rivian with the new account's email and password.

If Rivolt says it found no vehicle, the usual cause is an invite that has not been accepted yet. Finish step 4, then use Check again in Rivolt.
