# Rivolt Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

**Who runs Rivolt.** Rivolt (rivolt.dev) is run by **Anton Pohorilyi, an individual** (not a company) in Texas, United States ("we"). Contact: anton@rivolt.dev. Rivolt is independent and not affiliated with Rivian. This policy covers the hosted service only, not copies of the open-source code that others run themselves.

**Who it is for.** Adults (18 and over) in the United States who own or drive a Rivian.

**What we collect, and why**
- **Your account:** email, display name and your sign-in provider's account id, to sign you in; for each sign-in, the IP address and browser it came from, to keep you signed in and spot stolen sessions; we delete these 30 days after the sign-in expires or you sign out. If you asked for access, your request (email and message): deleted after 90 days if it is not approved, and if it is approved, once you sign up or 30 days after the invite expires unused.
- **Your Rivian sign-in:** your Rivian email and the session tokens Rivian issues, stored encrypted, so we can keep reading your vehicle. **Your Rivian password is never stored**; it is used once to sign in to Rivian.
- **Your vehicle's data**, read from Rivian: charge level, range, odometer, speed, temperatures, tire pressure, software version, trim and picture, and **GPS location**. We turn it into drives, charges and costs. Location is the most sensitive data we hold. It is never shown to other users. Rivolt records the vehicle, not the driver: if someone else drives it, their trips are recorded too.
- **Rivian charging receipts**, only if you turn them on in Settings (off by default; turning them off deletes the stored receipts): amount billed, currency, energy, times, vendor, city, and charger name (not for home chargers). We never ask for your payment card or address.
- **Your settings:** electricity rates, saved places and trips, notification and email preferences.
- **Notifications:** if you turn on notifications, your browser's push address and User-Agent.
- **Emails from us:** we send a welcome email and up to two setup reminders until Rivolt has recorded data from your truck; you can turn them off in Settings or with the link in the email.
- **Technical logs:** IP address, pages requested, errors, your account id, your vehicle's Rivian id and some raw vehicle data, to run and fix the service. Kept 90 days.
- **Product analytics:** our own simple usage events (no IP address, location, amounts or emails), with a random id stored in your browser. Kept 90 days. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, we record nothing.

**Who we share it with.** Only these, and only for the service:
- **Your sign-in provider (Google or GitHub)**, if you use it to sign in to Rivolt.
- **Rivian**, to read your vehicle's data. Rivolt's requests present themselves as Rivian's own mobile app (see the [Terms](/terms#2), section 2).
- **Cloudflare**, which every request to rivolt.dev passes through (including your IP address).
- **Resend**, which sends our emails (your email address, and your statement summary if you turn on the monthly statement email).
- **Your browser's push service**, if you turn on notifications.
- **Open-Meteo**, for weather (trip coordinates rounded to about 11 km, plus the hour) and, sometimes, for place search (the place you typed).
- **An AI provider (Anthropic, OpenAI or Google Gemini), only if you turn it on.** Trip recaps send no coordinates or addresses. Trip advice sends charger names, or locations rounded to about 11 km, and tire pressures; never the text you type.

We run our own servers in the United States, and database backups go to storage run inside the same cluster, not to a third-party cloud. As the operator, we can see stored data, and use that only to run, support and repair the service. We do not share your data with anyone else, except where the law requires it. **We do not sell your data, and there is no advertising** and no third-party analytics script.

**Keeping, exporting and deleting**
- If you connect a vehicle, we keep your history until you delete it.
- If you never connect Rivian, your account is deleted 120 days after signup. We email a warning at day 90, and never delete less than 30 days after that warning. This starts only after this policy is published.
- In Settings you can export your data, delete your history, or delete your whole account. Signing Rivolt out of Rivian deletes the stored Rivian session; you can also revoke access in the Rivian app.
- After you delete your account, copies can remain in logs for up to 90 days and in backups for up to 365 days. Analytics events not linked to your account cannot be found, so they simply expire after 90 days.

**Security.** Your Rivian session is encrypted, every user's data is kept separate, and the code is open source. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will email you without undue delay, and within 30 days of confirming it.

**Your choices.** Wherever you live, you can see, export, correct and delete your data, in the app or by emailing us; we answer within 30 days. Your data is stored and processed in the United States.

**Changes.** We will tell you about material changes by email and in the app at least 30 days before they take effect. We will ask for your consent before using data we already hold in a materially new way. If Rivolt moves to a company or a new owner, we will email you at least 30 days beforehand, so you can export or delete your data.
