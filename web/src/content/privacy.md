# Rivolt Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

**Who runs Rivolt.** Rivolt (rivolt.dev) is run by **ZeroDowntime LLC, a Texas limited liability company** ("we"). Contact: support@rivolt.dev. Rivolt is independent and not affiliated with Rivian. This policy covers the hosted service only, not copies of the open-source code that others run themselves.

**Who it is for.** Adults (18 and over) in the United States who own or drive a Rivian.

**What we collect, and why**
- **Your account:** email, display name and your sign-in provider's account id, to sign you in; for each sign-in, the IP address and browser it came from, to keep you signed in and spot stolen sessions (for the Rivolt iPhone app, the device type it reports, such as "iPhone", so you can recognise it in your list of signed-in devices and sign it out); we delete these 30 days after the sign-in expires or you sign out. If you asked for access, your request (email and message): deleted after 90 days if it is not approved, and if it is approved, once you sign up or 30 days after the invite expires unused.
- **Passkeys**, only if you add one in Settings: the passkey's credential id and public key, a counter that rises each time it is used, the kind of authenticator it reports (for example iCloud Keychain or a security key) and how it connects, whether it can be backed up, the name you give it, and when it was added and last used. Your account also has a random identifier that your device stores with the passkey. While you add a passkey or sign in with one, a short-lived challenge is kept for five minutes. The private key, and your fingerprint or face, stay on your device or in your password manager and never reach us. We email you when a passkey is added. Removing a passkey deletes that passkey's details; deleting your account deletes all of this, including the random identifier.
- **Your Rivian sign-in:** your Rivian email and the session tokens Rivian issues, stored encrypted, so we can keep reading your vehicle. **Your Rivian password is never stored**; it is used once to sign in to Rivian.
- **Your vehicle's data**, read from Rivian: charge level, range, odometer, speed, temperatures, tire pressure, software version, trim and picture, whether a trailer is connected, and **GPS location**. We turn it into drives, charges and costs. Location is the most sensitive data we hold. It is never shown to other users. Rivolt records the vehicle, not the driver: if someone else drives it, their trips are recorded too. Altitude on recorded drives comes from a copy of the public Terrarium elevation dataset (Mapzen, via AWS Open Data) that we download in bulk and host ourselves; no request about your drives leaves our servers.
- **Rivian charging receipts**, only if you turn them on in Settings (off by default; turning them off deletes the stored receipts): amount billed, currency, energy, times, vendor, city, and charger name (not for home chargers). We never ask for your payment card or address.
- **Your settings:** electricity rates, saved places, including your home location (as exact as you set it), and trips, notification and email preferences, and your time zone (for when reminders arrive).
- **Notifications:** if you turn on notifications, your browser's push address and User-Agent. Notifications you can turn on: charging complete, software updates, a plug-in reminder when the truck is parked at home below a level you choose, a left-open alert when a door, the frunk, the liftgate, the tailgate or the tonneau has stayed open for 15 minutes after the truck was parked and left (off until you turn it on; it uses only the truck's own reported state, names no place, and can be missed if the truck is offline), milestones, and a trip recap (off until you turn it on): a notification after you drive a saved trip, with its planned and actual arrival charge and cost; the trip's name is not in the notification. If you turn them on in the Rivolt iPhone app, we also keep your phone's Apple push address (a token Apple gives the app), which sign-in it belongs to, and your notification switches; we delete the address when you turn notifications off, sign the phone out, or Apple reports it unusable. The text of each notification is kept for 24 hours so your phone can fetch it, then deleted. If you also turn on live charging on the lock screen (off until you do), we keep the push address Apple gives the app for each charging display, until the charge ends, and at the latest 13 hours after it starts, and delete it sooner when you turn the switch off or sign the phone out.
- **Emails from us:** we send a welcome email and up to two setup reminders until Rivolt has recorded data from your truck; you can turn them off in Settings or with the link in the email. We also send a weekly summary if you turn it on.
- **Cards you share:** Cards you create to share are made on your device. Rivolt doesn't upload, post or track them.
- **Technical logs:** IP address, pages requested, errors, your account id, your vehicle's Rivian id and some raw vehicle data, to run and fix the service. Kept 90 days.
- **Product analytics:** our own simple usage events (no IP address, location, amounts or emails), with a random id stored in your browser. Kept 90 days. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, we record nothing.

**Who we share it with.** Only these, and only for the service:
- **Your sign-in provider (Google or GitHub)**, if you use it to sign in to Rivolt.
- **Rivian**, to read your vehicle's data. Rivolt's requests present themselves as Rivian's own mobile app (see the [Terms](/terms#2), section 2).
- **Cloudflare**, which every request to rivolt.dev passes through (including your IP address).
- **Resend**, which sends our emails (your email address, and your statement summary if you turn on the monthly statement email).
- **Your browser's push service**, if you turn on notifications.
- **Apple**, if you turn on notifications in the Rivolt iPhone app: Apple's push service gets your phone's push address and a random notification number with the generic line "Rivolt update". The words of the notification, which can mention your charge or your truck, never go through Apple; your phone fetches them from Rivolt. If you turn on live charging on the lock screen, Apple also receives the push address of that lock-screen display and, while your truck charges and once when it finishes, your charge level, charging power and minutes to your charge limit. It never receives your truck's location, name or VIN, or your Rivolt account.
- **Open-Meteo**, for weather (where a drive started, or a planned trip's route, rounded to about 11 km, plus the hour; or your truck's location if you turn on outside temperature) and, sometimes, for place search (the place you typed).
- **An AI provider (Anthropic, OpenAI or Google Gemini), only if you turn it on.** Drive analysis sends the drive's date and time, its figures (distance, speed, energy, battery, climb and descent), temperatures, weather, tire pressures and your vehicle details (tires, wheels, accessories, load, towing); never coordinates or place names. Trip advice sends charger names, or locations rounded to about 11 km, and tire pressures; never the text you type.
- **Anthropic (Claude), the AI tools we use to help run and repair Rivolt:** they may process stored data for that purpose only.

We run our own servers in the United States, and database backups go to storage run inside the same cluster, not to a third-party cloud. As the operator, we can see stored data, and use that only to run, support and repair the service. We use AI tools to help us run and repair Rivolt; they may process stored data for that purpose and nothing else. We do not share your data with anyone else, except where the law requires it. **We do not sell your data, and there is no advertising** and no third-party analytics script.

**Keeping, exporting and deleting**
- If you connect a vehicle, we keep your history until you delete it.
- A record of which reminders and summaries we sent (not their content) is kept about 13 months, so none is sent twice.
- If you never connect Rivian, your account is deleted 120 days after signup. We email a warning at day 90 to your verified address, and never delete less than 30 days after that warning. If we have no verified email address for you, there is no warning and the account is deleted at day 120. For accounts created before this line was published, the 120 days count from the day it was published.
- In Settings you can export your data, delete your history, or delete your whole account. Signing Rivolt out of Rivian deletes the stored Rivian session; you can also revoke access in the Rivian app.
- After you delete your account, copies can remain in logs for up to 90 days and in backups for up to 365 days. If you signed in with Apple and Apple could not be reached when you deleted it, we keep a pending request to Apple to revoke Rivolt's access (with the encrypted token it needs) until it succeeds, for 30 days at most. Analytics events not linked to your account cannot be found, so they simply expire after 90 days. Records of administrator actions on your account (what was done and when, no content) are kept for 2 years for security, including after you delete your account.

**Security.** Your Rivian session is encrypted, every user's data is kept separate, and the code is open source. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will email you without undue delay, and within 30 days of confirming it.

**Your choices.** Wherever you live, you can see, export, correct and delete your data, in the app or by emailing us; we answer within 30 days. Your data is stored and processed in the United States.

**Changes.** We will tell you about material changes by email and in the app at least 30 days before they take effect. We will ask for your consent before using data we already hold in a materially new way. If Rivolt moves to a company or a new owner, we will email you at least 30 days beforehand, so you can export or delete your data.
