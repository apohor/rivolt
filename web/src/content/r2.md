# Your first week with an R2 in Rivolt

Rivolt is a free, open-source companion for Rivian owners: it records your truck's drives and charges, prices every charge at your own rate, and plans trips. It is unofficial and not affiliated with Rivian. R2 support is new, so this page says exactly what is checked and what is not.

## Day 1: connect

Sign up at rivolt.dev, then connect your Rivian on the Connect step. We recommend creating a second Rivian account, adding it to your R2 as an Authorized Driver in the Rivian app, and connecting that one: removing Rivolt is then just removing the driver. Your Rivian password is used once to sign in and is never stored. If Rivolt says it found no vehicle, the usual cause is a driver invite that has not been accepted yet: sign in to the Rivian app on a phone with that account once, then tap Check again. Details: /safe-to-connect.

## What is recorded on the first R2s on Rivolt

Drives, charges and plug-in are recorded on the first R2s on Rivolt, along with charge limit, battery, range and odometer, location, cabin and battery temperature, tire pressure, sleep and power state, and the reported battery capacity. Time to full charge is shown as an estimate, from Rivian's own countdown. These are the fields the Truck tab's "what's checked" card lists for an R2.

## What is not on the R2 yet

Trip charge target, why a charge stopped, camp and pet modes, Rivian's parked-energy breakdown and smart-charging status are not shown for an R2. Each appears once it has been confirmed on an R2. Outside temperature doesn't come through for any truck; Rivolt can look it up from weather data if you turn that on (off by default).

## Days 2 to 7: what to look at

- Now: charge level, range and what the truck is doing. A charge-done notification is available if you turn it on (Settings → Notifications; on iPhone, add Rivolt to the home screen first or use the app).
- Activity: every drive with its route and charts; every charge with kWh, its cost (an estimate unless Rivian billed it) and the source of the price. Set your home rate in Settings → Charging so home charges are priced as they arrive.
- Money: the month's cost so far (estimates where a price was not billed), by home, away and fast charging.
- Truck: tire pressures with a plain "Low" below 32 psi or when the truck says so, and the sleep card. Sleep health needs five recorded days this week and fourteen before, so it reads "Not enough history yet" at first.

## Tell us what looks wrong

Open an R2 report on GitHub: github.com/apohor/rivolt/issues/new?template=r2-report.yml. It asks for the software version and what you saw; no VIN, no address, no location. "It just worked" is useful too.

Estimates are labelled as estimates. Rivolt is unofficial and not affiliated with Rivian; it uses Rivian's unofficial API, which can change at any time.
