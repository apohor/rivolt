# Data credits

**Weather.** Weather data by [Open-Meteo.com](https://open-meteo.com/), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). It is shown in the trip planner, on a drive's weather chart and as the outside temperature, and we may have changed it (rounded places, converted units, derived values such as headwind).

Open-Meteo's forecasts are built from the national weather services' models. We use:

- **NOAA** GFS and RRFS, from the United States National Weather Service (US government works).
- **Environment and Climate Change Canada** GEM. Contains information licensed under the [Open Government Licence – Canada](https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada).

Data processed by Open-Meteo.com. Rivolt is not endorsed by Open-Meteo, NOAA or Environment and Climate Change Canada.

**Maps and places.** Map data and place search use [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) contributors' data, under the Open Database Licence.

**Not affiliated.** Rivolt is unofficial and is not affiliated with Rivian.
