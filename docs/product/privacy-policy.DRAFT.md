## The short version
- **What we do:** we read your Rivian's data through your Rivian account and turn it into drives, charges and costs.
- **Your Rivian password is never stored.** We keep an encrypted sign-in session so we can keep reading your vehicle. Logging out of Rivian in Rivolt deletes it, and you can revoke access from the Rivian app (use an Authorized Driver account if you want a separate, revocable login).
- **We never send commands to your vehicle.** Rivolt only reads. Its only write-style requests to Rivian are the sign-in steps (CSRF token, Login, LoginWithOTP).
- **Location is the most sensitive thing we hold.** It is never shown to other users, and our public demo uses generated data only.
- **We do not sell your data and do not run advertising.** No third-party analytics scripts; our product analytics are first-party and honor Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control.
- **You can export and delete.** Download your data and delete your history or your whole account in Settings.
- **Retention:** if you connect a vehicle, we keep your history until you delete it. An account that never connected Rivian is deleted 120 days after signup, with an email warning at day 90 and never less than 30 days after that warning. Deleted data can stay in backups for up to 365 days (sections 5 and 9). Automatic deletion of unused accounts starts only after this policy is published.
- **Who it is for:** adults (18 and over) in the United States who own or drive a Rivian.

### Product analytics events (if enabled)
- **Random browser id.** Before you sign in, the browser stores a random id (`anon_id`) in localStorage and attaches it to events. This is a persistent identifier. It is not a cookie.
- **Linkable after sign-in.** Once you are signed in, each event row stores both `anon_id` and your `user_id`. Earlier events from the same browser (for example a landing-page view with its referrer host and utm_source) can therefore be linked to your account.
- **Retention.** Events are deleted after 90 days.
- **Do Not Track / Global Privacy Control.** If a request carries `DNT: 1` or `Sec-GPC: 1`, the server stores nothing (enforced server-side in `internal/api/events.go`).
- **Deletion.** Deleting your account removes events linked to your `user_id` (`ON DELETE CASCADE`). Events recorded only under `anon_id` have no `user_id`, so account deletion does not touch them and they cannot be tied to you. They are removed by an hourly purge of events older than 90 days, which runs at startup and every hour regardless of whether event collection is switched on. There is no other way to request deletion of anonymous-only events, because we cannot identify which are yours.

## 3. Location data
Location is the most sensitive data we hold. It is stored per vehicle sample and per drive/charge, and briefly in the live-state cache (section 2). It is used to classify charges as Home / Public / Fast and to show maps. It is never shown to other users. Rivolt records the vehicle, not the person: if someone else drives your vehicle, their trips and locations are recorded in your account too, so tell them (Terms, section 6). Positions rounded to about 1 km from the time-boxed log capture (section 2, ending 2026-10-20) stay in the logs for 90 days. Rivolt's public demo uses generated data only (a sample truck; no real vehicle). Shared statement images contain no coordinates or addresses **[VERIFY once U3 ships]**. The monthly statement email contains totals and a summary, not a map **[VERIFY template]**.

## 5. Retention, export and deletion
- **Retention.** **Accounts with a vehicle:** we keep vehicle samples, drives and charges until you delete them; there is no automatic deletion of history (owner decision), and partition drops are manual. Raw-sample retention (for example 24 months) may be added later and will be announced first. **Accounts that never connected Rivian** (owner decision, 2026-10-17): an account with no vehicle and no stored Rivian sign-in gets a warning email at day 90 and is deleted 120 days after signup, and never less than 30 days after the warning (`internal/db/inactive_purge.go`). Connecting Rivian cancels it; signing in does not. Admin and disabled accounts are not touched. Deletion is the same as "Delete everything" below. The job is built (#344, in v0.36.8 and later) and switched off by the `inactive_account_purge` flag; it is switched on only after this policy is published, so until then nothing is deleted automatically.
- **Logs.** Logs are kept for 90 days (Loki, 2160 h), set by our log stack rather than the app (default, owner may change). Deleting your account does not remove log lines already written; they age out after the 90 days. **[VERIFY: no per-user deletion path in Loki.]**
- **Export.** Signed-in users can download all their drives, charges and samples as one JSON bundle: `GET /api/data/backup` (`internal/api/data.go`), or a zip of CSVs (charges with effective cost source, drives, trips) plus settings from `GET /api/export` (Settings > Data > Export my data). The bundle includes settings. **[VERIFY: whether the exports include saved places and price overrides; receipts are included (`rivian_receipts.csv`).]**
- **Delete history only.** `DELETE /api/data/sessions` wipes samples, drives and charges, keeping your account and settings.
- **Delete everything.** `DELETE /api/data/account` deletes your account and cascades through per-user tables (drives, charges, samples, settings, user_secrets, push subscriptions, statement email preferences and send log, sessions, events), then asks the identity provider to remove your record (if that step fails it is recorded and an administrator retries it, so it is not silently lost). Last admin accounts are protected.
  - **Waitlist request.** Deleting your account also deletes your signup request (email and request message), matched by email or username, in the same transaction (A6, #81; `internal/db/account_delete.go`). **[VERIFY wording against `internal/api/data.go` at publication.]** If you asked for access but never created an account, email us and we will delete your request (section 2 for automatic retention).
- **Disconnect Rivian.** Logging out of Rivian in Rivolt deletes the sealed session. You can also revoke an Authorized Driver from the Rivian app.
- **Repair undo copies.** When an operator repairs bad records (for example a phantom charge), the deleted rows are copied to an undo table (`charges_deleted_backup`, migration 0049) kept for at least 90 days. Deleting your account removes your rows from that table (#207, deployed since v0.33.0).
- **Caches.** Redis live-state entries are keyed per vehicle and are not cleared by account deletion; they expire on their own (2 hours / 90 seconds, section 2).
- **Backups.** The database is backed up continuously (write-ahead log) with a daily base backup at 02:30 UTC, and backups are kept for **365 days** (CNPG `retentionPolicy: 365d`, `rivolt-infra/apps/platform/rivolt/values.yaml`). Data you delete can therefore remain in backups for up to a year; we do not edit backups. **[VERIFY: whether a restore procedure re-applies earlier deletions; if not, say so here.]** Whole-year retention is a default; the owner may shorten it (default, owner may change). **[DECIDE: the legal review recommends about 35 days of point-in-time backups, plus a list of deleted account ids that is re-applied after any restore, so a deletion cannot come back.]**

## 6. Security
Per-user scoping on every query and row-level security policies; opaque server-side sessions; envelope encryption for Rivian credentials; a kill switch for the upstream; open-source code you can audit. Vehicle history is protected by access control and storage encryption as configured, not by per-user encryption. No system is perfectly secure.

**If there is a breach.** If we confirm that someone without permission got access to personal data we hold about you, we will email you without undue delay, and within 30 days of confirming it, unless a law enforcement agency asks us in writing to wait. This covers all of your data, including location history and your Rivian sign-in session, even where breach-notice laws would not require it. The email will say what happened, what data was involved, what we have done, and what you can do (for example, signing Rivolt out of Rivian in Settings and revoking Rivolt's access in the Rivian app). Where a state law requires a shorter deadline, or notice to a regulator, we will meet it. **[ATTORNEY TO CONFIRM: the 30-day commitment (California, Colorado, Florida, New York and Washington set 30 days), the law-enforcement delay, and whether to promise notice for data the statutes do not cover.]**

## 7. Your choices and rights
You can, from inside the app: see and export your data, correct your settings and prices, delete your history, delete your account, turn off notifications and the statement email, and revoke Rivolt's access to your Rivian account from the Rivian app.

Rivolt is a free, non-commercial project run by one person for a small number of users. To use a right we do not offer in the app, email the contact in section 8; we respond within 30 days (default, owner may change).
