# Claim verification: "Rivian's app shows no charging cost" (P1)

Status 2026-10-13. Verdict: **cannot be stated as written.** A narrower claim is supportable.

## Evidence
| Finding | Source | Date |
|---|---|---|
| Owners requested "add your energy rates and have it calculate an est. cost per session"; thread unresolved, no Rivian reply | https://rivianforums.com/forum/threads/rivian-app-should-display-charging-session-cost.11297/ | Dec 2022 – Apr 2023 (stale) |
| The Energy app (2025.18, mobile app 3.1+) graphs "show you dynamic cost and time estimates" during charging | https://rivian.com/stories/software-update-2025-18 | 2025 |
| Vehicle screens show live charging cost at Tesla Superchargers | https://insideevs.com/news/713456/rivian-screens-display-live-charging-costs-tesla-superchargers/ (fetch failed; headline only) | 2023 |
| Charging history exists at Profile > Manage Account > Charging history, for sessions started in-app or on Rivian chargers; authorized drivers see only their own | https://rivian.com/support/article/where-can-i-view-my-charging-history (page body not retrievable; text from search snippet) | n/a |
| Public network prices are shown on the map site details | https://www.rivianownersforum.com/threads/ran-charging-costs.8951/ | n/a |

## What we could NOT confirm
- Whether charging history shows a per-session **cost** in 2026 (page body unavailable; forum evidence is 3 years old).
- Whether the app lets you enter a **home electricity rate**. No evidence either way for 2026.
- Whether the app offers a **monthly total or export**. No evidence found.

## Safe wording for the landing page
Do NOT write: "Rivian's app doesn't show charging cost."
DO write (each is supportable by Rivolt's own behaviour, not a claim about Rivian):
- "Every charge priced at *your* rate, with the source labelled."
- "A monthly statement and CSV export of what your truck cost."
- "Plan a trip's cost, then compare it with what you actually paid."

## To close before using any comparative claim
1. Owner opens the current Rivian app (Profile > Charging history) and screenshots one home and one DCFC session. Mask location.
2. Check the iOS release notes for "cost" / "rate": https://apps.apple.com/app/id1570215232
3. Re-run this check at each launch; Rivian ships monthly.
