**New**
- **Refunds you can do yourself:** a web Pro payment from the last 14 days, renewals included, can be refunded in Settings > Account > Plan (Refund and cancel, once per account); Pro ends right away.
- **Trip planning is part of Pro:** accounts created after November 27, 2026 plan trips with Pro. Accounts created on or before that day keep the single-day planner and three saved trips, and everyone's saved trips stay readable, exportable and deletable.
- **Sign in with Apple** is now listed on the landing page next to the other sign-in options.

**Fixed**
- **Place search:** places near you come first, hotels show their town, and hotels with chargers from the charger list say "EV charging". A far-away exact match (a city you typed in full) still shows.
- **Searching too fast:** the search box waits until you stop typing and starts at 3 characters; if you search a lot in a short time it says "Too many searches, wait a moment" instead of "unavailable".
- **Parked drain card (Truck page):** the sentence about time awake now gives the share of the parked loss that happened while the truck was awake, the same number as its bar (#2593).

**Changed**
- **Terms and Privacy** (updated November 27, 2026): refunds within 14 days, trip planning in Pro, and how Apple handles in-app purchases.
