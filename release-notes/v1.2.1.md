A small release of fixes. (v1.2.0 was tagged but never deployed; this replaces it.)

**Trip planner**
- On a computer, the rain radar legend now sits at the bottom of the map. It used to float next to the Radar button.

**Charging**
- The status at the top of the Now card shows the whole finish time. It is no longer cut off part-way through the time.
- "Time left" rounds up to the whole minute, so it agrees with the finish time.

**Everywhere**
- Temperatures read "86°F", without a space before the unit.
- On small phones with very large text, the top of the Now card wraps instead of being cut off.
