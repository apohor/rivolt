**Improvements**
- Round trip detail on the web draws every leg of a merged round trip, not just the first.
- Each stop gets a pin labelled "Parked N min", and a new "N legs" card lists every leg with its times, miles and parked minutes.
- The round trip share card says "Round trip" and shows the number of legs and the driving time.

**Fixes**
- A zero-distance stub is no longer counted as a leg. Its time goes into the parked minutes instead.
- Thanks to the owner, who reported on November 12 that a round trip showed only its first leg.
