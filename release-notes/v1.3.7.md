Fixes to what v1.3.6 shipped, and two small additions.

- Terms: the Terms page now lists its earlier versions (November 21, November 17 and October 23, 2026) with a link to each. The v1.3.6 notes said the earlier version was available from the Terms page; the November 21 and November 17 versions in fact opened only by direct address (October 23 was already linked).
- Pro tier: on a narrow screen with large text, the Trends range control (12 months, 24 months, All) no longer runs off the side; it wraps.
- Trip planner: a charger's power badge in the itinerary ("FAST · 200 kW") stays on one line.
- Self-hosted: operator notices (a new access request, a finished Rivian sign-in) can go to their own address with RIVOLT_EMAIL_OPERATOR_NOTICE_TO; unset, they go to the From address as before.
