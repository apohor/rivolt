**Now**
- While charging, Now shows power, time to target, energy and range added, rate, time elapsed and when it was plugged in.
- Cost so far is priced from your price book, and marked ≈ when it is an estimate.
- While driving, Now shows speed, gear and mode, distance, time, efficiency and energy.
- While parked, Now shows a map with the weather.
