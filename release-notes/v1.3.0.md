The Now card gets a graph for each thing your truck can be doing, and loses a few things that were in the way.

**Now**
- Parked or asleep: the battery over the last day, when the truck slept and woke, and how much it lost since it parked.
- Charging: the battery and power over the session, the pack temperature, and when it should finish.
- Driving: your speed and battery over the drive.
- The "Next 3 h" rain line is gone.
- Only the Live button opens Live. Tapping elsewhere on the card no longer takes you away.

**Small screens and large text**
- On narrow phones with very large text, figures and their units stay whole and inside the card, and the month chart's date labels no longer run into each other.
- The status chip beside "Now" no longer appears in the wrong place for a moment when the page opens.
