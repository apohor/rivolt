**Improvements**
- Gaps in GPS signal on a drive now show as a faded stretch of the route, not a solid line.
- Each drive has a one-line summary under its title.
- The Legs card on round trips starts collapsed. Tap it to open.
- Share cards and month and year cards follow your units: kilometres or miles, and °C or °F. The web year card now does too.
- Whole-hour stops read "2 h" instead of "2 h 0 min".
- Timeline labels no longer overlap on busy days.
