A simpler way in, a redesigned trip planner, and a charging pulse.

**New**
- **Sign-up:** accept the Terms on the sign-up screen, then add your truck or look around with a sample truck first.
- **Connect your Rivian:** one short form for your Rivian app login. A separate driver account is still offered as the safer option.
- **Trip planner:** the form folds to one line after you plan, the elevation and Save trip sit below the itinerary, and each charging stop is a ring that shows the charge you add there.
- **Trip planner:** start and destination have the same colours on the map and in the itinerary, and an arrival corrected for weather reads the same in the summary and the itinerary, with Rivian's own figure beside it.
- **Charging pulse:** while your truck charges, Now and Live show a live bolt and glow.

**Fixes**
- "Add my truck" from the sample truck opens the Rivian sign-in for someone who already has an account.

**Self-hosted**
- When Rivian sign-in is off on a server, people read that it isn't available right now, with Try again. It is on by default; RIVIAN_CLIENT=stub or mock turns the live client off.
- The new sign-up, planner and pulse are behind the design keys signup_v2, plan_v2 and charge_pulse.
