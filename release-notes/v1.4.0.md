The free tier's history window, a longer Trends view for the Pro tier, and fixes.

- Free tier: new accounts see the last 6 months of drives, charges and costs. Accounts created before November 22, 2026 keep the last 12 months. Nothing is deleted: everything stays stored, stays in the free export, and shows again with the Pro tier.
- The Terms were updated on November 22 to say so (section 5). The earlier version stays available from the Terms page.
- Pro tier: Trends can now show 12 months, 24 months or all of your history.
- Pro tier: the Pro sheet and the support page now list rain and snow likely at arrival on planned trips.
- Now: a fast charge no longer shows a cost estimated at your home rate while Rivian has not priced the session yet.
- The plan card, the Pro sheet and the limit notes take the free window's length from your account, so they always match what you see.
- Under the hood: old layouts that every account had already left were removed, which makes the app smaller to load.
