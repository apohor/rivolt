**Launch**
- Rivolt 1.0 is out. The web app at rivolt.dev is the full experience, and the iPhone app follows.
- Free tier: Now, Activity, Money and Truck for the last 12 months, the monthly statement with CSV downloads, single-day trip planning, share cards and every alert, plus a full export of everything Rivolt holds.
- Rivolt Pro adds unlimited history and trends, multi-day trip plans, forecast radar along the route and every vehicle on one screen. The free tier is never taken away.

**Improvements**
- Year cards on web and iPhone follow your distance and temperature units: kilometres or miles, and °C or °F.
- Gaps in GPS signal on a drive show as a faded stretch of the route instead of a dashed line.
- A drive's trailer setting is one line under its title, with a menu to change it.
- The Legs card on round trips starts collapsed. Tap it to open.
- Timeline labels no longer overlap on busy days, and whole-hour stops read "2 h".
