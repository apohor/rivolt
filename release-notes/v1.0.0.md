**Launch**
- Rivolt 1.0 is out on the web at rivolt.dev. The iPhone app is in TestFlight as iPhone 1.0.0.
- Free tier: Now, Activity, Money and Truck for the last 12 months, the monthly statement with CSV downloads, single-day trip planning, share cards and every alert, plus a full export of everything Rivolt holds.
- Rivolt Pro adds unlimited history and trends, multi-day trip plans with overnight stops, re-planning from where the truck is, and every vehicle shown.
